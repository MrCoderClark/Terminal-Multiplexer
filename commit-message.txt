M3 drive-by: harden the ConPTY echo round-trip test for CI

  The wait-then-drain pattern was racy on slow Windows runners.
  cmd.exe /c echo finishes almost immediately, and ConPTY's render
  pump runs in a separate process: when the child exits before the
  pump has rendered its output, ClosePseudoConsole drops whatever
  hasn't made it onto our output pipe yet. The 500 ms drain delay
  masked this locally but didn't on GitHub's windows-latest runner.

  Change the command to cmd /c "echo TOKEN & ping -n 2 127.0.0.1
  >nul" so cmd.exe stays alive for ~1 s after the echo, and swap the
  "wait for exit, then read" shape for a 15 s polling loop that
  succeeds the moment the token appears on the output pipe. Dropping
  the Pty at the end still forces the reader thread to EOF for a
  clean teardown.
